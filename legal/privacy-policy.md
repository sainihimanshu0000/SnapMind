# SnapMind Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

SnapMind (“we”, “the app”) is a personal screenshot organizer. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## 1. Summary

SnapMind is designed to keep your screenshots on your device. We do not operate a SnapMind cloud account that receives your images by default. Processing such as text reading (OCR) happens on-device when available.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, SnapMind may store locally:

- Screenshot image files you import or save
- OCR text extracted from those images
- Categories, tags, notes, favorites, collections, reminders
- App preferences (for example, whether screenshot quick actions are enabled)

## 3. Permissions

- **Photos / media images:** used to import screenshots you select and, only if you enable Screenshot Quick Actions, to detect new screenshots so you can save, dismiss, or delete them.
- **Notifications:** used for optional reminders and, if enabled, the Android screenshot watcher notification.
- **Background / boot:** used only when Screenshot Quick Actions is enabled, so watching can resume after reboot.

## 4. Sharing

SnapMind does not sell your personal information. Export and Share features only send data when you explicitly choose a destination (for example Files, Mail, or Drive) through the system share sheet.

## 5. Children

SnapMind is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to collect personal information from children.

## 6. Deletion

You can delete individual screenshots or use **Settings → Delete all data** to erase the local SnapMind library on this device. Uninstalling the app also removes app-private storage.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions about SnapMind, contact the developer listed on the Google Play store listing for this app.
